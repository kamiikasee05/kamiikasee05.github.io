T

TUSTOCK

Inventory and sales management for retail shops · SaaS

In production with one paying client

Problem

Kiosks, bookstores and grocery stores track inventory and sales by hand or in spreadsheets, with old hardware and, very often, without reliable internet. They needed a simple system that didn't depend on the cloud to run.

Solution

A system that runs 100% on the shop's PC, installs in 15 minutes and doesn't require a technician to use. It includes an Android app to scan codes with the phone camera and an optional cloud monitor to keep an eye on the shop remotely. Deliberately optimized for weak hardware (2 cores / 4 GB of RAM).

Stack

Python FastAPI SQLAlchemy SQLite (WAL) React 18 TypeScript Vite Kotlin / Android ML Kit · CameraX Cloud monitor (Railway)

Result

Product in production, installed in real shops and with one paying client. I took it end to end: code, installation, training and support. It works without internet, updates via USB drive and is designed so anyone can use it.

C

COMANDER

Order-management system for restaurants · product

Launch stage

Problem

Small venues take orders over WhatsApp and delivery apps: messages pile up during peak hours, the order gets written three times, and the customer's data stays in the app, not with the venue.

Solution

An order-management system that runs on the venue's computer: the counter takes the order, the kitchen receives it on a board with timers, the history logs it and the manager administers the menu and the promotions. No per-sale commission and no internet dependency to operate.

Stack

Next.js 16 React 19 TypeScript Prisma SQLite Tailwind CSS 4

Result

Complete, working product: counter, kitchen (KDS) with timers, history, menu admin, promotions and manager login, with a real burger restaurant's menu loaded for the demos. It's at launch stage: no signed clients yet, and I'll say it plainly — what exists is the system running and ready to try.
